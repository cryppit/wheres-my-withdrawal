Where's My Withdrawal

Paste the OP Mainnet, Base, Arbitrum One, Linea or ZKsync Era transaction that started your withdrawal to Ethereum. See whether it is done, what step is missing, when it opens, and who can do it.

Try:

Anyone can finish a withdrawal. On OP Mainnet and Base, proving and finalizing are open to any address; on Arbitrum One and Linea, so is the claim; on ZKsync Era, so is the finalize step, which ZKsync's own finalizer usually takes for you. The money only ever goes to the withdrawal's recipient.

Someone else may already have. Some services now prove and finish old withdrawals for others and then ask the owner for a tip. You owe nothing: the funds reach the recipient either way.

How it reads. Your browser asks public RPCs directly, at the latest finalized Ethereum block. No wallet, no keys, nothing signed. Source.
