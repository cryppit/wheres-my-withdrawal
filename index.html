A real result

Done

A 0.1 ETH Arbitrum One withdrawal, claimed on Ethereum by a third party. The money still went to its recipient.

Anyone can finish it

On OP Mainnet and Base, proving and finalizing are open to any address; on Arbitrum One, Robinhood Chain and Linea, so is the claim; on ZKsync Era, so is the finalize step, which ZKsync's own finalizer usually takes. The money only ever goes to the recipient.

You owe no tip

Some services now prove and finish old withdrawals for others, then ask the owner for a tip. The check names who sent each step. You owe nothing: the funds reach the recipient either way.

Read from Ethereum

Your browser asks public RPCs directly, pinned to the latest finalized Ethereum block, and every wait is read live from the contracts. No wallet, no keys, nothing signed, nothing stored.

What it tells apart

Every withdrawal gets one of these, from Ethereum's own contracts.

  • DoneFinalized or claimed; the money arrived.
  • ReadyReady to prove, finalize or claim: anyone, now.
  • WaitingA window or batch still running, with when it opens.
  • Needs a replayThe final message failed and must be sent again.
